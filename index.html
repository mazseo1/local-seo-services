FAQ

Questions before getting started?

Here are answers to the questions local business owners ask most often.

How long does a business website take?

Most standard projects can be completed quickly once the required business information, branding, images, and content are available. The exact timeline depends on the package and project scope.

Is hosting included?

This starter website is designed to be hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Your hosting/domain arrangement can be discussed separately depending on your requirements.

Does the $250 GMB plan guarantee rankings?

No ethical SEO service can guarantee a specific Google ranking. The monthly service focuses on improving your Google Business Profile, relevance, completeness, local signals, and ongoing optimization.

Can you build websites for any local business?

Yes. The structure can be adapted for service businesses such as movers, contractors, cleaners, salons, clinics, restaurants, agencies, home services, and many others.

Can I upgrade my website later?

Yes. You can start with a smaller package and expand the website, local SEO, content, and optimization as your business grows.
